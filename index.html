Definition

Who is OK1JDV?

OK1JDV is the callsign of David Veselý, an amateur radio operator from Žireč near Dvůr Králové nad Labem in the Czech Republic, who works exclusively CW on 20 and 40 metres and focuses on SOTA and IOTA activations using portable QRP equipment.

The home station is an IC-7300 with a DX Commander Signature 9 vertical, running HF DX CW. In the field it is an IC-705 running QRP.

The land-based part of the programme is SOTA activations with my son, primarily in the Krkonoše mountains. The maritime part is the Mediterranean Expedition: the yacht Pharaon as mother ship, dinghy landing on an island, station built on the shore.

Motto: Per Aspera Ad Astra.

How a landing works

  • The yacht anchors in the lee of the island and serves as mother ship.
  • The landing party goes ashore by dinghy with the IC-705, antenna and battery.
  • Station and antenna are built on the shore, then the activation runs in CW.
  • Exfiltration back to the yacht. No landing is made without cover from the boat.

Operation from the deck runs as OK1JDV/MM.

Training base

What is SOTA Camp?

SOTA Camp is a dedicated clearing in the small wood on the property by the house. It is a training base, not a garden seating area.

Its purpose is training the combination of bushcraft skills and HF/VHF portable QRP operation — both at once, because on a summit they also arrive at once.

What is practised there

  • Building and tuning an antenna in the field
  • Battery power and energy budgeting
  • Fast deployment and teardown of the station
  • Logging in the field
  • Operating in rain, wind and winter

The logic is simple: an activation is not won on the summit. On the summit you only execute what you have already rehearsed. A SOTA activation is not about the radio, it is about getting a working station up the hill and back down.

I do not publish coordinates or a site plan.
